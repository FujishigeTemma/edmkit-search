## The problem

You have a multivariate time series `X` of shape `(T, D)` and a target `Y` you want to predict from `X`. The question is *not* "which model fits `Y` best?" but **which subset of columns of `X` carries the predictive information about `Y`?**

This question recurs throughout empirical dynamic modeling (EDM): identifying state-space coordinates, ranking causal drivers, pruning a high-dimensional candidate pool. New to EDM? Start with [edmkit's *What is EDM?*](https://fujishigetemma.github.io/edmkit/concepts/edm/).

edmkit-search turns the subset question into one loop on top of [`edmkit`](https://github.com/FujishigeTemma/edmkit)'s batched simplex projection and S-Map: grow column subsets, score each by out-of-sample predictive skill, keep the best survivors, and inspect the train-versus-validation curve.

## Three orthogonal pieces

The loop decomposes into three plain callables. Swap any one and the other two keep working.

| Axis | What it controls | Built-ins |
| ---- | ---------------- | --------- |
| **Neighborhood** | how each state expands into children | `forward(n)` |
| **Energy** | how children are scored | `holdout`, `loo`, `folds` |
| **Strategy** | which children survive each step | `greedy`, `beam(width=W)` |

A `Dataset` wraps `(X, Y)`. `state.initial()` seeds the search at the empty subset. `strategy.run` drives the loop and yields one frontier per step — the trajectory itself is the output.

## Why edmkit-search?

## What you'll do in 10 minutes

The [Getting Started](/edmkit-search/getting-started/) tutorial walks through a complete experiment on a Lorenz-96 toy dataset in seven steps:

1. Frame "higher is better" predictive skill as an energy ("lower is better").
2. Wrap the data and carve an **outer fold** for honest evaluation.
3. Pre-filter weak candidates with [`edmkit.embedding.scan`](https://fujishigetemma.github.io/edmkit/reference/embedding/).
4. Build the energy from an **inner fold** of the training arm.
5. Compose neighborhood + strategy and run the search.
6. Read the training-vs-validation trace as a diagnostic curve.
7. Sweep `(metric, energy, strategy)` configurations and compare.

By the end, the pieces compose into a script you can adapt to a real dataset — see [Validation](/edmkit-search/concepts/validation/) for the sweep template.
